# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This is a project-authored abstraction over the bounded observations in this batch.

- Marketplaces, retail, banking, and mobility products all need an entry orientation, but the governing dimension differs: country, storefront market, product role, or service scope.
- Brand propositions and market-specific offerings remain non-transferable. Only the structural need to orient a visitor is a candidate for comparison.
- Each product has only one direct state in this batch. None is eligible for comparative synthesis until it reaches five distinct direct states.

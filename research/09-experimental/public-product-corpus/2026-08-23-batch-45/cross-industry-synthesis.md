# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and AI feature evidence from team communication, database workspace, design collaboration, and creative editing products.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages continue to serve as feature explainers;
- AI is increasingly attached to credit allowances or plan-specific access;
- free or trial entry points remain prominent for conversion;
- seat and plan-type language is used to reduce upgrade ambiguity.

Differences worth preserving:

- communication tools emphasize message history and AI tools;
- workspace tools emphasize automations and credit spend;
- design tools emphasize seats and shared AI credits;
- creative tools emphasize trials and creative AI access.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

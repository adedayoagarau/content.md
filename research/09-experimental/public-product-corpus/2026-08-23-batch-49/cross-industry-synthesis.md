# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and eligibility evidence from design, workspace, work-management, and storage products.

Observed tendencies:

- AI usage limits are increasingly described in plan-specific terms;
- education, nonprofit, and startup eligibility are used as conversion levers;
- credits and usage caps are part of the customer mental model;
- family and shared-storage plans need plain-language ownership framing.

Differences worth preserving:

- design tools emphasize AI usage and education eligibility;
- workspace tools emphasize credits and agent features;
- work-management tools emphasize nonprofit and AI Studio access;
- storage tools emphasize family sharing and basic plans.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

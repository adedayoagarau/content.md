# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This is a project-authored abstraction over the bounded observations in this batch.

Observed tendencies:
- Entry surfaces lead with the user's immediate category, fulfillment, switching, or service-value question.
- Core actions label the first meaningful decision: search, choose, compare plans, or explore local availability.
- Outcome language is context-specific: shipping visibility, same-day fulfillment, financial incentives, and provider switching are not interchangeable claims.

Transfer boundaries:
- Availability gates and incentive language depend on product eligibility, market, and terms; the observed wording is not reusable copy.
- The bounded signed-out pages did not expose error, recovery, account, or destructive moments.

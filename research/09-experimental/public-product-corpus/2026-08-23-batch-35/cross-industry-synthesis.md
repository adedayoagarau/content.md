# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds high-value pricing and support material from video meetings, signature workflows, design collaboration, and work management products.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often act as product explainers, not just cost tables;
- video meetings pair plan choice with AI features and time limits;
- signature workflows clarify free-tier caps and team-plan boundaries;
- design collaboration products explain shared AI credits across seats;
- work-management products pair nonprofit eligibility with plan structure.

Differences worth preserving:

- video tools emphasize AI and meeting duration;
- signature tools emphasize request caps and team scaling;
- design tools emphasize shared AI credits and add-ons;
- work tools emphasize eligibility and starter-plan framing.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds high-value pricing and support material from knowledge workspaces, work platforms, creative suites, and team communication products.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often act as product explainers, not just cost tables;
- knowledge workspaces pair plan choice with AI feature scope;
- work platforms clarify credits, tiers, and support boundaries before purchase;
- creative suites pair pricing with eligibility framing for education and nonprofit use;
- chat platforms keep the free-to-paid distinction easy to compare.

Differences worth preserving:

- knowledge tools emphasize AI workspace scope;
- work tools emphasize credits and consumption;
- creative tools emphasize audience eligibility and premium access;
- chat tools emphasize upgrade thresholds and retained history.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

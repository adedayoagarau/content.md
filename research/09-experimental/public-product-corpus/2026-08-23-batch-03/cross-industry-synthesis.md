# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch broadens the corpus into delivery, logistics, entertainment, insurance, telecom, and additional marketplace systems.

Observed tendencies:

- role-based entry pages make the user choose a path early;
- plan pages often pair a headline with a reassurance or constraint;
- community and media products lean on scale metaphors and broad access claims;
- support pages can expose recovery or management language without requiring login;
- simple public homepages still often collapse to a single identity word or short phrase.

Differences worth preserving:

- insurance and telecom are more explicit about coverage, eligibility, and setup;
- delivery platforms use worker-recruitment and operational language;
- travel marketplaces mix consumer browsing with partner/listing flows;
- entertainment products tend to highlight emotional or habitual consumption rather than functional task completion.

This batch remains evidence-only and does not create controlled-corpus eligibility.

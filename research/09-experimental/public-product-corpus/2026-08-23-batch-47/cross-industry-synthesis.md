# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and eligibility evidence from support, work-management, cloud-storage, and content-management products.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages are increasingly explicit about what usage triggers charges;
- eligibility language and discounts sit alongside core plan information;
- family and personal plan structures need simple ownership language;
- AI consumption meters are becoming part of the plan story.

Differences worth preserving:

- support tools emphasize outcome-based billing;
- work-management tools emphasize nonprofit and AI Studio access;
- cloud-storage tools emphasize family plans and personal plan returns;
- content-management tools emphasize AI units and personal upgrade paths.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds the corpus’s clearest progress and destructive-state language so far.

Observed tendencies:

- progress states usually combine a present-tense status with a specific recovery or next step;
- cancellation and disconnect flows are far more direct than acquisition flows;
- exports and order/payment states often include explicit deadlines or status windows;
- security products can rely on named recovery artifacts rather than generic instructions;
- public admin and partner surfaces are especially rich in permission and workflow language.

Differences worth preserving:

- commerce and productivity product states talk about order/payment/export workflows;
- telecom cancellation is procedural and often requires guided contact;
- security recovery emphasizes artifacts and responsibility;
- partnership dashboards expose access and admin concepts that don’t appear on consumer homepages.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

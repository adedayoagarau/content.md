# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds high-value pricing and support material from video meetings, knowledge workspaces, work management, and creative education products.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often act as product explainers, not just cost tables;
- video meeting pages connect AI features to meeting limits and plan tiers;
- knowledge workspace pages link credits to plan scope;
- work management pages make free-tier boundaries obvious before upgrade;
- creative education pages explain premium access for K-12 users.

Differences worth preserving:

- meeting tools emphasize AI and session limits;
- workspace tools emphasize credits and plan inclusion;
- work tools emphasize free tiers and plan separation;
- creative tools emphasize education eligibility and premium access.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

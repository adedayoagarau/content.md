# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds the most useful missing material so far: support, recovery, and troubleshooting language.

Observed tendencies:

- support pages expose the route to help before they explain every topic;
- recovery pages are more direct than marketing pages, often naming an error and a next step;
- some products still keep support and billing language public even when account access is required later;
- public self-serve help often ends with an explicit human escalation path;
- a visible support flow is usually more actionable than a homepage for state modeling.

Differences worth preserving:

- collaboration tools focus on team support, login, and communication issues;
- telecom pages emphasize billing, contact channels, and service management;
- travel partner pages emphasize assistance and complaint handling;
- developer docs emphasize reconnecting, logs, and environment recovery.

This batch is evidence-only and does not create controlled-corpus eligibility.

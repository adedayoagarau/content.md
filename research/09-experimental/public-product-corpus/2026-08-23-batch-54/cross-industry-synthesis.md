# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and AI evidence from team communication, database workspace, site building, and video messaging products.

Observed tendencies:

- free plans remain the main onboarding anchor;
- AI credits and usage meters are increasingly common in public plan pages;
- billing language is more understandable when paired with the feature bundle;
- trials still matter for converting AI-enhanced plans.

Differences worth preserving:

- team communication emphasizes AI-powered work and upgrade paths;
- workspace products emphasize credit and billing explanations;
- site builders emphasize workspace plans and reset cadence;
- video products emphasize trials and AI feature bundles.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and plan evidence from customer service, work management, cloud storage, and content management products.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often double as product explainers, not just cost tables;
- AI features are increasingly tied to plan scope or outcome-based pricing;
- free or personal plans are still used as important conversion anchors;
- eligibility and plan-type language reduce confusion before upgrade decisions.

Differences worth preserving:

- customer service products emphasize support channels and AI agent outcomes;
- work management products emphasize AI credits and student discounts;
- cloud storage products emphasize personal-vs-team plan separation;
- content management products emphasize free single-user access and file limits.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

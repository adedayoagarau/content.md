# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds high-value pricing and support material from visual collaboration, team communication, knowledge workspace, and work management products.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often act as product explainers, not just cost tables;
- visual collaboration pages pair AI features with board access and sharing limits;
- team communication pages make the free-to-paid transition obvious;
- knowledge workspace pages link AI scope to the selected plan;
- work-management pages explain credits and plan tiers before purchase.

Differences worth preserving:

- visual tools emphasize access and collaboration depth;
- communication tools emphasize upgrade thresholds and retained history;
- workspace tools emphasize AI scope and plan inclusion;
- work tools emphasize credits and tiering.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

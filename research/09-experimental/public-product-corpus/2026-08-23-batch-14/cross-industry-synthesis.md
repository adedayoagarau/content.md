# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds high-value pricing and support material from creative suites, developer platforms, and marketing tools.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often act as product explainers, not just cost tables;
- support pages reinforce account, password, and billing language already implied in pricing;
- developer billing increasingly uses credits or usage accounting;
- creative suites and marketing tools expose plan names, contact limits, or trial terms to help users self-select;
- public homepages continue to compress a product into a short identity phrase or CTA.

Differences worth preserving:

- creative software emphasizes membership, subscriptions, and support;
- developer platforms emphasize credits, usage, and runtime billing;
- commerce platforms emphasize enterprise tiers and sales handoff;
- marketing tools emphasize contact limits and audience scale.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

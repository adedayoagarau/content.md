# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds high-value pricing and support material from customer service, file sharing, database tooling, and developer platforms.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often act as product explainers, not just cost tables;
- customer service pages connect AI outcomes to value and pricing;
- file-sharing pages explain free-tier scope before upgrade;
- database pages show how add-ons layer on top of the base plan;
- developer pages separate core hosting from AI add-ons and compute.

Differences worth preserving:

- service tools emphasize outcomes and value;
- file tools emphasize free scope and collaboration;
- database tools emphasize add-ons and plan layering;
- developer tools emphasize core platform vs. AI extras.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

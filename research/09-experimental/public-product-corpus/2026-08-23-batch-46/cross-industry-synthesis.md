# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and AI feature evidence from developer, collaboration, site-building, and video messaging products.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often explain usage meters, credits, or plan limits;
- free plans remain a critical entry point across categories;
- AI is commonly bundled into paid tiers or tracked as credits;
- upgrade language is clearer when plan names and feature bundles are paired together.

Differences worth preserving:

- developer tools emphasize usage-based billing and credit conversion;
- collaboration tools emphasize free-to-premium upgrade boundaries;
- site builders emphasize workspace and site plan coupling;
- video tools emphasize AI enhancement plus recording and editing workflows.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

# Cross-Industry Synthesis

Across the first batch, public product content clusters into a few reusable evidence patterns:

- entry pages usually compress value proposition, category identity, and next-step CTA into one screen;
- commitment moments shift to fees, trials, or account creation language;
- guidance-heavy public services emphasize routing, eligibility, or help-path selection;
- design-system sites use abstract vocabulary to explain reusable components and patterns;
- error text is often sparse on public pages, so many failure states remain `not_observed`.

Important differences:

- health and government pages carry a stronger urgency or eligibility burden than commerce or creative tools;
- finance pages surface cost, contract, or trial language much more directly than general-purpose platforms;
- developer and design-system sites are more taxonomic and less transactional.

This batch is evidence-only and does not claim approved voice, universal reuse, or competitive superiority.

# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This second batch broadens the corpus beyond checkout and public-service flows into banking, education, identity, social networking, and AI assistants.

Observed tendencies:

- homepages often carry the shortest possible product promise;
- pricing pages turn the promise into a tiered commitment;
- login pages shift from marketing to continuation language;
- role-based entry pages are common in hosting, identity, and AI products;
- public product pages rarely expose meaningful recovery states without a live interaction.

Differences worth preserving:

- banking and security pages are more explicit about trust, fees, and access;
- education pages surface credential and course structure;
- AI pages combine capability breadth with unusually broad task framing;
- social products use community and identity language rather than feature lists.

Evidence remains public-only and nonconforming for controlled corpus use.

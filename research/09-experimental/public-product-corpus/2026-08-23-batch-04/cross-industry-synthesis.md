# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds more collaboration, identity, and commerce coverage, which makes the corpus less home-page heavy and more useful for state modeling.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages are the densest source of commitment language;
- docs pages transform product value into setup instructions;
- homepages still frequently reduce the product to one sentence or title;
- support and help pages are where recovery language appears most clearly in public view;
- public product systems often expose only the happy path and plan selection, leaving error and destructive states sparse.

Differences worth preserving:

- collaboration tools emphasize team, workspace, and customizable home concepts;
- identity tools emphasize secure access, docs, and implementation readiness;
- commerce systems emphasize tiering, sales handoff, and business scale;
- creative tools emphasize the breadth of capabilities rather than a single job.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

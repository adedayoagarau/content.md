# Targeted remediation attestation

The deterministic automation assessment checks all 30 revised mappings against
their immutable evidence digests, raw structural signatures, closed taxonomy
IDs, counterexamples, remediation lineage, remaining evidence gaps, and authority
boundaries. It provides recommendations, not reviewer decisions.

Each reviewer independently opens their remediation assignment alongside
`taxonomy-remediation-automation-assessment.json`, inspects any mappings they do
not accept, and records their own checklist results, rationale, decision, and
completed assignment digest. Reviewers must not attest for one another.

No receipt, taxonomy-version approval, or v0.2 activation is implied by the
automation assessment or this handoff.

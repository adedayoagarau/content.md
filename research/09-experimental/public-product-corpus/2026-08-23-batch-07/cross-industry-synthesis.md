# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds another layer of breadth across communities, ride-hailing, and AI pricing.

Observed tendencies:

- community products often describe themselves as a place or world to gather;
- ride-hailing mixes rider and driver role language, then quickly pivots to action;
- AI products increasingly explain plans in terms of credits, seats, or usage limits;
- social and marketing products use strong top-level metaphors before specifics;
- even where the page is public, the richest state language tends to appear in pricing and role-specific flows.

Differences worth preserving:

- social and community products emphasize belonging and discovery;
- ride-hailing and labor-adjacent products emphasize earnings and motion;
- AI products emphasize capacity and entitlement;
- marketing platforms emphasize contact count and audience scale.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

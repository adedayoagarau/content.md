# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and AI-economics evidence from workspace, whiteboarding, work-management, and task-management products.

Observed tendencies:

- AI access is increasingly described through plan gates, credit buckets, and trial language;
- pricing pages do a lot of explanatory work for both entry and success states;
- credit replenishment and upgrade prompts are often embedded directly in the page copy;
- plan matrices remain the main place where customers can compare AI value.

Differences worth preserving:

- workspace products emphasize plan eligibility and agent pricing;
- whiteboarding products emphasize credit caps and AI workflows;
- work-management products emphasize add-on credits and per-plan allowances;
- task-management products emphasize AI features as part of the upgrade story.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and AI evidence from developer, collaboration, site-building, and video messaging products.

Observed tendencies:

- free plans are still prominent as onboarding anchors;
- AI credits and AI trial language reduce ambiguity around paid features;
- upgrades are clearer when the base plan is spelled out;
- plan ladders often describe both the feature and the usage meter.

Differences worth preserving:

- developer platforms emphasize credits and model access;
- collaboration tools emphasize AI and automation;
- site builders emphasize workspace-level AI credit metering;
- video tools emphasize free starts and AI trial access.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

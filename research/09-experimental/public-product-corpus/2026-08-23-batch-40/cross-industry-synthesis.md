# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds high-value pricing and support material from video meetings, knowledge workspaces, work management, and content management products.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often act as product explainers, not just cost tables;
- video meeting pages connect AI features to meeting limits and plan tiers;
- knowledge workspace pages link AI scope to the selected plan;
- work management pages make free and paid tiers obvious before upgrade;
- content management pages pair free-account scope with security messaging.

Differences worth preserving:

- meeting tools emphasize AI and session limits;
- workspace tools emphasize AI scope and plan inclusion;
- work tools emphasize tier separation;
- content tools emphasize free scope and security.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

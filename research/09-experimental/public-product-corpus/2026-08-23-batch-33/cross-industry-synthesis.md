# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds high-value pricing and support material from video suites, work management, database tooling, and customer platforms.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often act as product explainers, not just cost tables;
- video suites use broad platform pages alongside AI feature pages to narrow the audience;
- work management pages clarify guest access and plan limits before the buyer commits;
- database and customer-platform pages use credits language to frame usage and value;
- pricing pages keep compressing the product into a short identity phrase or CTA.

Differences worth preserving:

- video tools emphasize AI and workflow acceleration;
- work tools emphasize roles, collaborators, and metered usage;
- database tools emphasize included AI allowances and billing clarity;
- customer platforms emphasize credit language and plan comparison.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

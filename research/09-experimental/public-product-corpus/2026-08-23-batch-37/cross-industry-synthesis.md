# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds high-value pricing and support material from developer platforms, work management, content management, and site building products.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often act as product explainers, not just cost tables;
- developer platform pages separate core hosting from AI add-ons and compute metering;
- work-management pages make nonprofit discounts and tier boundaries obvious;
- content-management pages clarify free-account boundaries before purchase;
- site-builder pages distinguish creation from hosted site-plan rules.

Differences worth preserving:

- developer tools emphasize add-ons and infrastructure cost;
- work tools emphasize eligibility and tiering;
- content tools emphasize free-plan scope and security;
- site tools emphasize hosting and plan separation.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

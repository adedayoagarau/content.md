# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds high-value pricing and support material from incident communications, publishing tools, video suites, and commerce platforms.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often act as product explainers, not just cost tables;
- publishing help pages reinforce the difference between a page, a site, and a domain;
- video suites use broad platform pages alongside sector-specific paths to narrow the audience;
- incident communications pages combine public/private scope with plan language to make visibility explicit;
- commerce pages continue to compress a product into a short identity phrase or CTA.

Differences worth preserving:

- incident tools emphasize audience scope, notifications, and public availability;
- publishing tools emphasize site publication and add-on boundaries;
- video suites emphasize cross-product platform scope and sector fit;
- commerce platforms emphasize self-serve plans plus enterprise terms.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

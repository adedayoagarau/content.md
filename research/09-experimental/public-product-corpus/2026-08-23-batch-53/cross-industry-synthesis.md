# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and eligibility evidence from developer, work-management, storage, and content-management products.

Observed tendencies:

- free tiers remain a key entry point for developer and storage products;
- AI studio and AI unit language continues to appear in pricing pages;
- family-plan and nonprofit eligibility remain important conversion levers;
- paid tiers are easier to compare when the public page shows the usage meter.

Differences worth preserving:

- developer platforms emphasize credits and model access;
- work-management tools emphasize AI Studio and nonprofit discounts;
- storage tools emphasize family plans and basic plans;
- content-management tools emphasize AI units and personal upgrades.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

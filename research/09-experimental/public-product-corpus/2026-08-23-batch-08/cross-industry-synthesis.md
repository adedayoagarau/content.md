# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds a more balanced mix of public-service, health, crypto, BNPL, delivery, and streaming pages.

Observed tendencies:

- tracking flows depend on a reference, order state, or application milestone;
- public health sites pair authority with browseable topic access;
- payment and delivery products increasingly blend order management with flexible payment language;
- support pages around finance and crypto make the user sign in or identify their account for better service;
- streaming offers still use short trial language and explicit term limits.

Differences worth preserving:

- government services use eligibility and reference-based progression;
- health agencies foreground trust and public-service authority;
- crypto products combine support, investor relations, and consumer purchase entry;
- BNPL and delivery products talk in terms of verification, tracking, and status.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

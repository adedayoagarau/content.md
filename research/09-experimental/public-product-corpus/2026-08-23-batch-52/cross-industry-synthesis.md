# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and eligibility evidence from developer, work-management, cloud-storage, and content-management products.

Observed tendencies:

- free plans and free trial paths remain important entry points;
- plan ladders increasingly explain AI credits or AI units explicitly;
- eligibility language for nonprofits and households stays prominent;
- paid plans are easier to compare when consumption metrics are visible.

Differences worth preserving:

- developer platforms emphasize credits and model access;
- work-management tools emphasize nonprofit and AI Studio availability;
- storage tools emphasize family plans and basic accounts;
- content-management tools emphasize AI units and platform add-ons.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

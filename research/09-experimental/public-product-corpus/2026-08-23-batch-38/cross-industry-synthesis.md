# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds high-value pricing and support material from customer service, project tracking, creative licensing, and content management products.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages often act as product explainers, not just cost tables;
- customer service pages connect AI outcomes to value and pricing;
- project-tracking pages separate free and paid capability boundaries;
- creative licensing pages distinguish free content from premium access;
- content-management pages pair free-account scope with security messaging.

Differences worth preserving:

- service tools emphasize outcomes and value;
- project tools emphasize tier separation;
- creative tools emphasize licensing and premium access;
- content tools emphasize free scope and security.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

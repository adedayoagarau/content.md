# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and eligibility evidence from design, creative, messaging, and workspace products.

Observed tendencies:

- plan pages remain the main surface for explaining AI credits and usage caps;
- education eligibility continues to be prominent for creative tools;
- paid messaging plans increasingly surface AI features alongside free-to-paid comparison;
- workspace billing is easier to understand when the credit meter is explicit.

Differences worth preserving:

- design products emphasize seats and shared AI credits;
- creative products emphasize education eligibility and AI usage limits;
- messaging products emphasize AI-powered work and upgrade paths;
- workspace products emphasize billing meters and credit cadences.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

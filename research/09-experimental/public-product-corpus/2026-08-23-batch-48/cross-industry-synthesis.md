# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and AI feature evidence from AI assistants, whiteboards, video communications, and creative editing products.

Observed tendencies:

- pricing pages continue to explain credits and plan ladders;
- AI is presented as a differentiator across collaboration tools;
- free entry plans remain important for onboarding;
- bundled AI terms are used to reduce feature ambiguity.

Differences worth preserving:

- assistant tools emphasize credits and plan boundaries;
- whiteboards emphasize team scale and AI-enabled collaboration;
- video tools emphasize AI companion plus team plans;
- creative tools emphasize free entry plus generative AI.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

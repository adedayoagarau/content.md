# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This batch adds public pricing and eligibility evidence from design, creative, whiteboard, and workspace products.

Observed tendencies:

- AI credits and AI usage limits are becoming common pricing terms;
- education access and startup offers continue to anchor conversions;
- plan ladders are clearer when seat or credit structures are visible;
- product pages increasingly bundle AI value with plan eligibility.

Differences worth preserving:

- design collaboration emphasizes seats and shared AI credits;
- creative tools emphasize education eligibility and AI usage caps;
- whiteboards emphasize team scale and AI help;
- workspace tools emphasize credits and startup offers.

This batch remains evidence-only and nonconforming for controlled-corpus promotion.

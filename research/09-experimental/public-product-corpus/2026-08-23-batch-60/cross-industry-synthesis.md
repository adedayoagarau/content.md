# Cross-Industry Synthesis

This is a project-authored abstraction over the bounded observations in this batch.

- Public care and public-service surfaces lead with access routing; neither provides a basis for treatment, eligibility, or outcome claims.
- Security products use a short capability bridge before routing to solution, setup, or support information.
- The Signal domain was policy-blocked and was not bypassed. Transactional, account, error, and recovery states remain unobserved.

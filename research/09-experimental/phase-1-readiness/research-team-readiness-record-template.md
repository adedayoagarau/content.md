# Research-team readiness record template

## Authority boundary

**[Proposal]** This template structures the P1-05 evidence required before synthetic operational rehearsal or participant collection. It assigns no person and establishes no qualification, access, authority, training completion, independence, or specialist capacity.

Current disposition: `not-for-use`. Every controlled-role assignment and evidence reference is `not-established`. The controlled-role labels below are not names, owner IDs, accounts, or claims that a role exists.

## Controlled rehearsal sequence

These steps describe a future authorized run. They are not permission to execute.

1. **Read back scope.** Operator and observer independently confirm the exact decision, synthetic-only boundary, tool manifest, versions, allowed records, disallowed operations, stop rule, and cleanup plan. Any mismatch stops the run.
2. **Preflight the environment.** Verify no participant/external data is present or reachable; unrelated windows and integrations are closed; capture, recording, transcription, model/AI, telemetry, network, and automated features match the exact approved disposition; and only the synthetic partitions are accessible.
3. **Materialize synthetic test objects.** Create only the approved synthetic focal record, prohibited-content marker, and derivative chain. Record versions/hashes and prove they contain no real person, organization, product, account, credential, or external artifact.
4. **Start independent observation.** The observer begins the monotonic time and state log. The operator reads each scripted boundary rather than improvising a participant account.
5. **Run branches R1 through R7.** Follow the matrix below. Record actual state transitions, stop behavior, record effects, unexpected behavior, timing, and failures without changing expected results after seeing them.
6. **Complete pointer-only handoff.** Sender and receiver independently verify controls, provenance, evidence axes, access classes, lifecycle, and next allowed step. An unresolved field must produce quarantine and rejection, not silent repair.
7. **Exercise deletion propagation.** Delete or approved-redact the disposable synthetic chain through every test partition, cache, export, processor, and backup class declared by the approved environment. Record per-system result and residual scan.
8. **Lock observations independently.** Operator and observer finalize separate basis, timing, branch, reflexivity, and failure records before discussion.
9. **Compare and disposition.** Preserve disagreement, adjudicate only under the declared procedure, record limitations, and decide whether the operational evidence supports revision or a bounded readiness reconsideration.
10. **Clean and verify.** Confirm no test object remains outside the permitted minimal audit boundary, revoke temporary access, restore the declared environment, and record independent cleanup verification.

## Stop conditions

Stop, preserve the bounded failure record, and begin the approved cleanup path if:

- a precondition, version, decision, controlled role, or tool state is absent, stale, incompatible, or cannot be independently verified;
- real participant, personal, proprietary, customer, employee, credential, secret, account, or external-source data appears or becomes reachable;
- recording, capture, transcription, model, telemetry, network, connector, or persistence behavior exceeds the exact approved boundary;
- the prohibited-content marker cannot be stopped and quarantined without copying it;
- withdrawal/deletion cannot reach every declared linkable derivative or residuals cannot be verified;
- sender and receiver cannot reject an incomplete handoff;
- an operator must invent participant behavior, consent, meaning, authority, approval, or result to continue; or
- cleanup or access revocation cannot be proved.

## Bounded exit criteria

A future rehearsal record can support reconsidering P1-06 only if it proves, with two independently locked observations:

- two trained researchers used the exact approved versions and synthetic-only materials;
- recording and automated capture stayed off by default;
- stop, quarantine, access restriction, safe resume or termination, and metadata-only incident logging operated as specified;
- withdrawal/deletion propagated through the complete declared synthetic lineage and a residual scan was performed;
- pointer-only handoff, rejection, and quarantine worked without duplication or silent repair;
- state transitions and immutable changes were captured;
- the full timing trace was recorded without compressing consent, safety, playback, or close;
- all failures, disagreements, limitations, and environment-specific assumptions remain visible; and
- cleanup and access revocation were independently verified.

Even such a result would be evidence about one synthetic operational condition, not participant comprehension, research validity, accessibility, language quality, system suitability, product behavior, or permission to recruit. P1-B would still require its own current governance, materials, processing, recruitment, and gate records. This runbook remains `not-for-use` regardless of any future controlled rehearsal record.

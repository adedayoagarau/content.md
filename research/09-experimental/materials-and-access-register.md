# Materials and access acquisition register

## Result and authority boundary

**[Proposal]** Use this register to decide what must exist before each experimental phase starts, who is qualified to provide or approve it, the least-privilege form that is sufficient, and the evidence that closes the dependency.

This file is an acquisition plan, not an acquisition event. It records no contact attempt, recruitment, participant consent, account creation, purchase, installation, download, token, connector authorization, product access, data-processing approval, retention approval, translation approval, or permission to operate Computer Use. A row marked `authorization-required` is not a request for a credential and is not authority to obtain or use one. Every external action still needs its applicable study approval, participant permission, provider agreement, capability gate, exact task grant, and action-time user authorization.

The current-state evidence cutoff is 19 August 2026. “Present” means present in this local research workspace at that cutoff; it does not mean validated, approved, implemented, licensed, externally available, or safe to use. Linked research artifacts retain their own source and evidence cutoffs, so adding a local development or environment artifact does not silently refresh an older external-source claim.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `present-local` | The exact local protocol, design, or working evidence exists and is linked. It may still be proposed or nonconforming. |
| `specified-not-materialized` | Required shape, fields, counts, or controls are defined, but the usable asset or implementation does not exist. |
| `missing` | No qualifying material, person, approval, environment, or record is evidenced in this workspace. |
| `blocked-access` | A direct preflight found that the required safe environment or access condition was unavailable. |
| `authorization-required` | The item must not be obtained or used until the named owner and applicable control path approve the exact scope. |
| `conditional` | Needed only if the study takes the named recording, model, translation, connector, locale, or product path. |
| `deferred` | Deliberately outside the current phase; absence narrows later claims rather than being silently ignored. |

`present-local` is never a substitute for participant consent, organizational authorization, specialist qualification, licensing, a current capability grant, or a successful test.

### Phase 1 gate sequence

The P1-B transition has three non-interchangeable records: a frozen inactive route candidate, a gate decision on that exact candidate version, and a later activation record that binds the unchanged candidate and passing gate to the exact operator and boundary. A passing gate decision alone does not authorize outreach. If the candidate, operator, channel, text, handoff, or data boundary changes, the activation is invalid and the sequence restarts; no record may be backdated.

| Gate | What it permits | Required evidence before transition | Evidence that belongs later and cannot be required here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1-A — Acquisition preparation starts | Internal owner identification, governance/processing review, drafting participant materials and instruments, staffing, and synthetic rehearsal. It permits no practitioner outreach, screening, collection, artifact request, account access, or credential request | This register and linked protocols are versioned; the proposed study scope, no-outreach boundary, owner types, and missing dependencies are visible. Any actual internal approval or staffing action follows its normal authorization route; this document does not perform it | Participant records, consent decisions, artifact plans, artifact observations, decision records, or pilot findings |
| P1-B — Recruitment and screening may start only after route activation | After the separate activation record exists: approved outreach, prescreen notice, minimum screen, scheduling intake, language/accommodation question, and broad artifact-class/feasibility question only. The gate decision by itself permits none of these | First freeze an inactive/no-outreach route candidate with exact operator class, channel, text, handoff, data boundary, version, and integrity reference. The P1-B gate then reviews that exact candidate. Only afterward may a distinct activation record bind the passing gate to the identical candidate and exact operational scope | Five recruited participants; participant-specific full consent; artifact contents; an artifact-backed finding; session notes or coding |
| P1-C — One participant's session may start | The exact scheduled 70-minute pilot session. Collection begins only after opening reconfirmation; artifact viewing begins only after its separate permission and final authority/redaction check | The participant passes the approved screen and matches an open pilot cell; P1-03 full packet was delivered with review time and current choices were recorded; P1-04 pre-session artifact class/plan passes without artifact acquisition; P1-05 roles/access pass; P1-06 environment/stop/quarantine rehearsal passes; P1-07 covers every intended session data class; P1-08 passes or records a justified not-applicable disposition; P1-09 session instruments/IDs freeze. | P1-D — Post-session record and Phase 1 pilot exit | Evidence recording, independent coding, adjudication, deletion/incident checks, five-session coverage review, and a decision to proceed, revise, or rerun | For each session: actual access modes, consent at event time, basis log, artifact observation result or explicit absence, any separately permitted extract/recording, independent locked coding, disagreement, retention/deletion, incident, and withdrawal state are recorded. Phase 1 acquisition preparation may therefore begin without pretending that participants, consent, or artifacts already exist. Recruitment cannot begin until the full candidate → P1-B gate decision → activation sequence completes; a gate result alone is insufficient. A session cannot begin until its P1-C gate; and `artifact_backed: true` or `threshold_eligible: true` cannot be assigned until the applicable P1-D evidence exists. Failure on consent, safety, unitization, or record-family separation requires protocol revision and a new bounded pilot; it cannot be waived by high participant yield.

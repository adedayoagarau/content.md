## Orthogonal evidence dimensions

Record all five dimensions independently for the exact evidence item and declared use. Do not replace them with one `confidence`, `quality`, or `status` value, and do not infer decision approval, governing applicability, implementation, release, or evaluation from them.

- **Observation strength:** `unobserved` / `observed` / `corroborated`
- **Challenge:** `undisputed` / `disputed`; link every material conflicting-evidence record
- **Freshness:** `current` / `stale`; include the determination date, declared use/scope, and review trigger
- **Lineage:** `active` / `superseded`; include the successor evidence-record ID when superseded
- **Epistemic qualifier:** `none` / `inferred` / `assumed`; state the inference or assumption and its validation need

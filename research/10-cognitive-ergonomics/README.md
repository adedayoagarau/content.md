# Cognitive ergonomics and behavioral influence workstream

## Purpose and boundary

This workstream investigates how cognition, behavior, accessibility, culture, and influence should inform content-design decisions before, during, and after writing. It converts the supplied research brief into a durable program of evidence review, operational modeling, adversarial evaluation, and practitioner validation.

It is not a collection of psychology tips, a behavioral-optimization playbook, or permission to infer an individual's mental or emotional state. A repeated claim remains a contextual hypothesis until its source, population, task, outcome, replication or corroboration, moderators, harms, accessibility and cultural limits, product-specific validation, accountable owner, semantic decision state, and exact approval record are known and kept separate.

No deterministic `CONTENT.md` rule, lint rule, personalization policy, behavioral intervention, or product mutation is authorized by this directory. Research outputs remain proposed until the evidence and approval gates below pass.

## Research domains

The workstream covers these independently scoped domains:

1. attention and perception;
2. memory and cognitive load;
3. recognition versus recall;
4. mental models and sensemaking;
5. information scent and wayfinding;
6. choice and decision architecture;
7. motivation, progress, and feedback;
8. emotion, stress, uncertainty, and trust;
9. social influence, social proof, and authority;
10. persuasion and elaboration;
11. defaults, reversibility, and user control;
12. habit formation and behavioral triggers;
13. research-response and observation biases;
14. cognitive accessibility;
15. cultural, linguistic, and localization boundaries; and
16. deceptive and manipulative design.

An industry label never supplies a cognitive model, tone rule, or influence permission. Applicability depends on the exact user goal, task, state, consequence, population, surface, language, locale, accessibility context, product behavior, evidence, and governing constraints.

## Scientific-claim hygiene

The source and claim layers must remain separate. A source record describes the material and how it was accessed. A claim record describes what can be asserted from it. A governing instrument, accountable owner, authorized approver, semantic decision, delivery state, and evaluation remain separately linked records.

Every psychology-claim record must contain:

- the exact claim and construct;
- original source IDs and source types;
- the separate evidence class or qualifier—such as primary empirical study, meta-analysis, narrative/conceptual review, semi-systematic review, or primary theory/model—without treating it as the canonical source type or claim label;
- population and sample;
- task, environment, comparison, and intervention;
- measured outcome and effect representation;
- replication, corroboration, contradiction, or null-result status;
- moderators and boundary conditions;
- cultural, linguistic, localization, and accessibility limits;
- foreseeable harms and misuse paths;
- bounded safe content-design applications;
- prohibited or high-risk applications;
- product-specific validation needed;
- freshness and review trigger; and
- independent semantic decision and approval records.

Contradictory findings remain visible. A meta-analysis does not establish universal applicability; an original experiment does not automatically generalize to a product; replication is not inferred from repeated citation; neuroscience vocabulary does not raise evidence quality; and a practitioner article is never ingested as normative instruction.

## Pre-writing decision boundary

Before proposing content, the future context compiler must determine or surface as unknown:

- the user's task, decision, prior knowledge, likely terminology, and relevant mental model evidence;
- what must be remembered across steps and which information is material;
- the cost, reversibility, and affected actors of misunderstanding;
- task-level evidence of urgency, uncertainty, stress, emotional strain, literacy, numeracy, language, or accessibility needs;
- the number, differentiation, consequence, and reversibility of choices;
- whether simplification removes incidental effort or meaningful control;
- whether defaults, progress, scarcity, social proof, and authority claims are factual, visible, reversible, and appropriately scoped;
- whether the experience supports the user's goal and informed choice rather than only an organizational metric; and
- the method that could evaluate comprehension, decision quality, effort, autonomy, recovery, and harm.

The system must never diagnose a person's cognitive state, emotion, vulnerability, intent, literacy, disability, culture, or susceptibility. It must not infer any such individual state from behavioral, language, device, time, demographic, accessibility-setting, or model-derived proxies. An explicit user-declared access need or preference may support only a separately governed, purpose-limited protective adaptation with minimization, reversibility, and the required controls; it is never evidence for persuasive targeting. Missing evidence produces an unknown, research request, safe default, or escalation—not a fabricated persona.

## Non-negotiable guardrails

The agent must not:

- apply a psychology claim as a universal law;
- infer vulnerability for persuasion or personalize persuasion using an inferred emotional or cognitive vulnerability under any claimed evidence or authorization; a separately proposed protective use must follow the bounded route above and must not increase persuasive pressure;
- fabricate or exaggerate social proof, scarcity, urgency, authority, progress, or consensus;
- hide material information through peripheral presentation, timing, or progressive disclosure;
- use a default that silently changes consent, price, privacy, eligibility, commitment, or another material consequence;
- treat clicks, conversion, attention, completion, or recall as proof of comprehension or informed choice;
- assume fewer choices are always better;
- assign universal meaning to color, shape, reading pattern, gesture, metaphor, or emotion;
- build habit loops around compulsion, anxiety, boredom, shame, fear, or fear of missing out; or
- let a business outcome compensate for failure in truthfulness, non-deception, material comprehension, valid consent, accessibility, user autonomy, or safety.

High-risk cases require the applicable product, research, accessibility, locale/culture, privacy, legal, ethics, safeguarding, or domain owner. Evidence and review do not themselves grant mutation, publication, enforcement, or experimentation capability.

## Evaluation model

Do not compute one psychology score. Preserve at least these independent outcomes:

- findability;
- comprehension;
- recall;
- decision quality;
- task success;
- recovery success;
- actual and perceived cognitive effort;
- accessibility;
- trust calibration;
- emotional appropriateness;
- user autonomy;
- reversibility;
- business outcome; and
- potential harm.

Truthfulness, non-deception, material comprehension, valid consent, accessibility, user autonomy, and safety are non-compensable. A conversion increase cannot outweigh their failure.

## Evidence-to-rule promotion

**[Proposal]** A conclusion may remain:

1. `research-hypothesis` when evidence or applicability is unresolved;
2. `advisory-candidate` when the evidence supports a bounded question or review prompt;
3. `deterministic-candidate` only when the observable condition and expected disposition can be evaluated without psychological inference; or
4. `approved-deterministic-rule` only after the exact rule, scope, counterexamples, false-positive tests, owner, semantic approval, current evidence, and enforcement eligibility are present.

Examples of potentially deterministic checks include missing material terms beside a preselected option, a countdown with no source record, or an unlabelled recurring charge. “The user feels anxious,” “this color creates trust,” or “three choices are optimal” can never become deterministic findings from interface text alone.

## Completion gate

This workstream remains open until all of the following are true:

- every normative rule has traceable fit evidence and scoped applicability;
- practitioner guidance is distinguishable from scientific evidence;
- populations, tasks, outcomes, moderators, contradictions, and null findings remain visible;
- accessibility, cultural, linguistic, localization, and high-risk limits are recorded;
- influence, persuasion, assistance, coercion, and manipulation are operationally distinguishable;
- every automated rule has counterexamples and false-positive tests;
- every high-risk intervention has the required qualified human decision and approval;
- product-specific claims have been tested with appropriate users and methods; and
- no article or study has been treated as raw model-training truth.

Completion additionally inherits the repository's evidence, decision, delivery, evaluation, capability, privacy, security, and approval boundaries. Producing every planned document is necessary but not sufficient to close the workstream.

## Seed-source handling

The supplied commercial, practitioner, and popular-press links are discovery seeds. They may identify a claim worth tracing, a phrase worth debunking, or a practice worth testing. They cannot establish a normative rule, source hierarchy, replication result, governing applicability, or product permission. Every retained scientific claim must be reconnected to the strongest available original, review, standards, or regulatory evidence and keep the seed's limitations visible where the seed itself is discussed.

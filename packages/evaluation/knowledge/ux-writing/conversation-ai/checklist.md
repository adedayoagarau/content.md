# Conversational and AI Interaction Checklist

Use this checklist to review an exchange, not an isolated utterance. A checked item records inspected evidence; it does not certify compliance or authorize release.

## 1. Evidence and Authority

- [ ] Approved sources define the task, state, tools, claims, and permissions.
- [ ] The accountable operator and content owner are known.
- [ ] Turn origin is classified as deterministic, retrieved, generated, human, or hybrid.
- [ ] Missing facts and assumptions are explicit.
- [ ] High-risk domain, privacy, security, accessibility, and locale owners are identified.
- [ ] No book heuristic is treated as current law, policy, platform behavior, or model capability.

## 2. Turn Contract

- [ ] Current state, speaker, addressee, intent, and next states are resolved.
- [ ] The turn addresses the participant's current goal.
- [ ] Questions collect only information needed for the disclosed task.
- [ ] Prior corrections and unresolved questions remain visible.
- [ ] Topic or goal changes are explicit and optional.
- [ ] Interruption, resumption, reset, and exit are defined.

## 3. Identity and Capability

- [ ] “I,” “we,” and role nouns have one evidenced referent.
- [ ] Automation and human involvement are represented truthfully when material.
- [ ] Capability statements match available tasks, tools, sources, and permissions.
- [ ] The system does not claim feelings, consciousness, relationship, or unsupported professional judgment.
- [ ] Persona traits do not expand authority.
- [ ] Human review state is not invented.

## 4. Grounding and Uncertainty

- [ ] Every material claim maps to an approved source or authoritative state.
- [ ] Source scope, currency, qualifications, and conflicts are preserved.
- [ ] Known, inferred, estimated, conflicting, missing, and unresolved claims are distinguished.
- [ ] Uncertainty explains its consequence and a safe next action.
- [ ] Citations support the claims they appear to support.
- [ ] Unsupported material claims are removed or escalated.

## 5. Tools and Actions

- [ ] Proposed, requested, authorized, started, pending, partial, complete, failed, and unknown states remain distinct.
- [ ] The turn claims success only from authoritative result state.
- [ ] Unknown outcomes do not invite harmful retry.
- [ ] Consequential actions preserve confirmation, cancellation, or correction as required.
- [ ] Tool failure retains relevant participant input.
- [ ] The responsible recovery owner is known.

## 6. Privacy, Memory, and Shared Devices

- [ ] Current speaker, account user, subject, and audience are distinguished.
- [ ] Sensitive content is not exposed before access and audience are resolved.
- [ ] Display, audio, transcript, notification, and handoff exposure are reviewed.
- [ ] Stored fields, purpose, duration, reuse, and recipients are evidenced.
- [ ] Inspect, correct, delete, reset, and nonuse controls exist where governed.
- [ ] Personalization does not rely on unsupported sensitive inference.

## 7. Repair and Handoff

- [ ] Misunderstanding is restated without blaming the participant.
- [ ] Preserved work and lost work are identified.
- [ ] A changed recovery path is available after repeated failure.
- [ ] Human destination, availability, expected timing, and fallback are verified.
- [ ] The participant controls which conversation context is transferred where required.
- [ ] Handoff status is not overstated.
- [ ] Appeal, remedy, or specialist escalation appears for consequential disputes.

## 8. Cross-Channel Behavior

- [ ] Material identity, status, uncertainty, consequence, and repair survive each supported mode.
- [ ] Spoken turns avoid unnecessary sensitive disclosure to bystanders.
- [ ] Transcript and history behavior are defined.
- [ ] Programmatic semantics, focus, announcements, keyboard, speech, captions, and fallback are verified as applicable.
- [ ] Locale, reading direction, pronunciation, morphology, and material parity are reviewed.
- [ ] Craft checks such as reading aloud are not treated as accessibility proof.

## 9. Evaluation Disposition

- [ ] Deterministic findings cite approved evidence and rule IDs.
- [ ] Material unknowns cause evidence requests and abstention.
- [ ] Advisory findings cannot override hard outcomes.
- [ ] Specialist questions name the required discipline and decision.
- [ ] Research questions name population, task, adverse context, and outcome.
- [ ] Hard failures block personality and tone ranking.
- [ ] Repaired turns are evaluated in sequence against the same rule-pack version.

## 10. Adverse Scenarios

- [ ] Ambiguous request or competing goals.
- [ ] Stale, missing, or conflicting source evidence.
- [ ] Tool timeout, partial success, or unknown transaction state.
- [ ] Shared account, household device, delegate, or bystander.
- [ ] Sensitive inference or surprise cross-session memory.
- [ ] Human queue unavailable or context transfer fails.
- [ ] High-risk request outside capability.
- [ ] Repeat use, interruption, correction, and abandonment.

# Voice and Expression Examples

All scenarios and interface text are original synthetic material. They demonstrate evaluation logic, not approved production copy.

## Concision that changes consequence

**Governed meaning:** Removing a shared workspace immediately removes access for 14 members; recovery is available for seven days.

**Bad concise candidate**

> Delete workspace?
>
> Delete

**Invariant-preserving candidate**

> Delete the Marigold workspace?
>
> Its 14 members will lose access immediately. An owner can restore it for seven days.
>
> Delete workspace

**Why it works:** The expression retains object, affected parties, timing, consequence, authority, and recovery. It is longer because the material contract is larger.

## Positive tone that obscures denial

**Bad**

> Good news—there are other opportunities! Your housing request wasn’t approved this time.

**Better**

> Your housing request was denied because the submitted income record is outside the program period.
>
> Review the income record
>
> Appeal this decision

**Why it works:** It prioritizes state, supported reason, and remedy. A domain specialist must approve the criterion and appeal wording.

## Humor in a consequential error

**Bad**

> Whoops! Your tax filing took a little vacation 😅

**Better**

> We can’t confirm whether your filing was submitted. Don’t submit it again while we check its status.
>
> Check filing status

**Why it works:** It removes trivializing character and preserves uncertainty plus safe action.

## Unsupported empathy claim

**Bad**

> I understand how frightening this result feels.

**Better**

> This screening result needs review by a clinician.
>
> Contact the clinical team

**Why it works:** The system does not claim emotion, understanding, or professional interpretation. The approved service contract must define the human route.

## Truthful automated-system identity

**Bad**

> I reviewed your case and decided you qualify.

**Better**

> An automated eligibility check matched the details you submitted to the current program rules. A caseworker must approve the result before enrollment.

**Why it works:** It identifies automation, evidence scope, provisional state, and human authority.

## Familiar wording that corrupts a governed term

**Governed term:** `authorized representative` has a defined policy meaning.

**Bad simplification**

> Add a helper

**Better**

> Add an authorized representative
>
> This person can act on your account within the permissions you choose.

**Why it works:** It keeps the governed term and adds an explanation rather than replacing it with an ambiguous role.

## Mechanics scoped to surface and locale

**Approved source-locale button:** `Save delivery address`

**Bad universal rule**

> Convert every locale to English title case: `Save Delivery Address`

**Correct evaluation:** `unknown` for other locales until locale-specific mechanics and translations are approved. Casing is an expression rule, not a cross-language semantic truth.

## Personality after hard planes pass

**Context:** A low-risk garden planner confirms an undoable color-theme change. The approved voice permits light playfulness, and research includes repeat use.

**Neutral candidate**

> Theme changed to Meadow. Undo

**Optional expressive candidate**

> Meadow mode is on. Undo

**Why both may pass:** They preserve the same state and recovery. Preference testing may select one; neither gains authority from sounding more distinctive.

## Warm apology with no remedy

**Candidate**

> We’re deeply sorry your reservation disappeared. You deserve better.

**Correct evaluation:** `fail`. The copy adds relational intensity but no state explanation, restoration, replacement, refund, or support action. The missing product remedy must be defined before expression review.

## Localization preserves invariant, not idiom

**Source meaning:** A person may cancel without a fee until 18:00 local time on 4 November.

**Unsafe approach:** Translate an informal “back out anytime today” idiom literally.

**Required approach:** Preserve cancellation right, no-fee condition, exact local deadline, date interpretation, and action route; a locale specialist chooses the expression and verifies cultural and jurisdictional fit.

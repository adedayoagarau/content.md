# Voice and Expression Rules

These are proposal-level candidate rules. They require approved project evidence and do not activate enforcement by themselves.

## Deterministic hard-rule candidates

### VE-H01 — Preserve every semantic invariant

Each expression must preserve the governed actor, fact, certainty, state, action, object, option, consequence, destination, timing, recovery, remedy, and operator identity that apply.

An unexplained invariant addition, omission, or change fails even when the result sounds clearer or more on-brand.

### VE-H02 — Do not invent authority or capability

Expression must not add expertise, permission, certainty, policy, source, human review, understanding, emotion, relationship, or system capability that evidence does not establish.

### VE-H03 — Keep system identity truthful

Automation, operator, data use, generated or retrieved status, capability limits, and human recourse remain explicit when material. “I,” “we,” “advisor,” “expert,” “partner,” or similar roles require resolved referents and authority.

### VE-H04 — Preserve material information

Brevity, simplification, formatting, or progressive disclosure must not remove or delay information that could change consent, cost, eligibility, safety, consequence, commitment, option, recovery, or remedy.

### VE-H05 — Use approved terminology in governed scope

When a concept has an approved scoped term, candidates must use it or an explicitly permitted variant. A familiar substitute that changes legal, policy, medical, financial, technical, identity, or product meaning fails.

### VE-H06 — Apply mechanics by locale and surface

Mechanics rules must identify organization, locale, channel, component, and version. Do not enforce universal English casing, punctuation, numerals, abbreviations, or length rules across unsupported scopes.

### VE-H07 — Derive tone from verified context

Tone must not rely on invented emotion, vulnerability, urgency, intimacy, or cultural effect. High-consequence states require verified context and declared tone boundaries.

### VE-H08 — Prohibit coercive expression

Reject unsupported guilt, shame, scarcity, urgency, relational pressure, or emotional leverage, especially around consent, payment, cancellation, necessary services, appeals, or privacy choices.

### VE-H09 — Keep optional character removable

Humor, idiom, emoji, character, and audible affect must not carry essential meaning, action, state, warning, or recovery. Removing them must leave a complete interaction contract.

### VE-H10 — Evaluate hard planes before style

Do not score or rank voice, tone, concision, warmth, humor, rhythm, or distinctiveness until authority, fidelity, state, safety, accessibility structure, material information, agency, recovery, and remedy pass.

## Deterministic outcomes

| Outcome | Use when |
|---|---|
| `pass` | Approved scoped evidence exists and the rule is satisfied |
| `fail` | Evidence establishes semantic drift or a prohibited expression |
| `unknown` | Material context, authority, locale, or invariant evidence is absent or conflicting |
| `not_applicable` | Declared applicability conditions are false |

Every result should retain rule ID, invariant or token identifier, evidence references, reason code, and repair requirement. Identical canonical inputs and rule versions should yield identical results and ordering.

## Advisory guidance

- Prefer concrete actors, verbs, objects, and consequences when they aid the task.
- Use familiar terms only when evidence supports familiarity for the intended population.
- Remove repetition and filler after verifying that no material meaning is lost.
- Explain necessary specialist terms rather than substituting imprecise language.
- Adjust acknowledgment to consequence and responsibility; avoid generic cheerfulness.
- Keep personality consistent with the approved role, channel, repetition, and locale.
- Treat reading aloud as an editing aid only.

## Specialist escalation

Escalate high-risk claims, denials, warnings, consent, remedies, sensitive identity terminology, stereotype risk, cultural adaptation, accessibility semantics, and regulated mechanics. Do not produce decision-ready expression when operator identity, professional authority, locale effect, or material source meaning is unresolved.

## Research and preference criteria

Research should test interpretation, comprehension, trust calibration, repetition, stress, offense, and cross-cultural effect. Only after hard and specialist gates pass may reviewers prefer warmth, humor, cadence, personality, or aesthetic economy.

# Voice and Expression Checklist

Use this checklist after the interaction context is assembled and before selecting a stylistic candidate.

## 1. Evidence and invariants

- [ ] Identify authoritative facts, sources, state, action effects, options, consequences, recovery, and remedy.
- [ ] List every applicable semantic invariant.
- [ ] Resolve speaker, operator, actor, owner, subject, recipient, and affected party.
- [ ] Record certainty, scope, currency, qualification, and unresolved evidence.
- [ ] Produce an expression-to-meaning diff for each candidate.
- [ ] Abstain when missing evidence could materially change meaning or harm.

## 2. Hard-before-soft gate

- [ ] Verify authority and evidence sufficiency.
- [ ] Verify factual and semantic fidelity.
- [ ] Verify product state and action accuracy.
- [ ] Verify safety, domain authority, privacy, security, consent, and nondiscrimination.
- [ ] Verify accessibility and localization structure.
- [ ] Verify material information, agency, consequence, recovery, and remedy.
- [ ] Stop style ranking if any applicable hard plane fails or is materially unknown.

## 3. Terminology and mechanics

- [ ] Use approved scoped terms or explicitly permitted variants.
- [ ] Explain necessary specialist terms without changing their meaning.
- [ ] Apply mechanics for the correct organization, locale, surface, component, and version.
- [ ] Avoid universal English word-count, casing, punctuation, reading-level, or tone rules.
- [ ] Verify dates, numbers, units, abbreviations, and names with locale authority.

## 4. Voice and identity

- [ ] Reference an approved, evidence-linked voice record and owner.
- [ ] Express voice through observable choices and boundaries.
- [ ] Keep automation, generation, retrieval, data use, capability, and human recourse truthful when material.
- [ ] Resolve every “I,” “we,” “you,” “your,” advisor, expert, or partner referent.
- [ ] Do not claim feelings, consciousness, empathy, listening, understanding, friendship, or professional authority without evidence.

## 5. Situational tone

- [ ] Base tone on verified state, consequence, responsibility, certainty, urgency, agency, power, prior effort, channel, and locale.
- [ ] Do not infer emotion, vulnerability, intimacy, or cultural effect without authority.
- [ ] Keep denial, loss, debt, illness, danger, and organization fault direct and proportionate.
- [ ] Avoid guilt, shame, unsupported urgency or scarcity, and relational pressure.
- [ ] Ensure acknowledgment does not replace action or remedy.

## 6. Clarity and concision

- [ ] Prefer concrete actors, verbs, objects, and consequences where they aid the task.
- [ ] Use familiar terms only when population evidence supports familiarity.
- [ ] Remove repetition and filler only after a material-information audit.
- [ ] Layer supporting detail without hiding decision-changing facts.
- [ ] Evaluate sufficient information for the task and risk, not shortest length.

## 7. Warmth, humor, and personality

- [ ] Confirm the expression layer is explicitly approved for this context and locale.
- [ ] Remove humor, idiom, emoji, sound, or character and verify the contract remains complete.
- [ ] Test first use, repetition, interruption, stress, and adverse contexts.
- [ ] Escalate stereotype, sensitive identity, cultural, or high-risk emotional concerns.
- [ ] Treat warmth, humor, rhythm, and distinctiveness as preferences after hard planes pass.

## 8. Evaluation evidence

- [ ] Record deterministic results as `pass`, `fail`, `unknown`, or `not_applicable` with rule and evidence IDs.
- [ ] Obtain specialist review for governed domain, accessibility, locale, and identity concerns.
- [ ] Research comprehension, interpretation, trust calibration, offense, and cross-cultural effect with intended populations.
- [ ] Do not treat conversion, engagement, preference, or reading aloud as proof of truth, accessibility, or user benefit.

## Exit criteria

A decision-ready expression preserves every semantic invariant, uses approved terminology and mechanics, represents identity and capability truthfully, and passes all hard and specialist gates. Style preferences may then distinguish among otherwise valid candidates; they never compensate for a failed contract.

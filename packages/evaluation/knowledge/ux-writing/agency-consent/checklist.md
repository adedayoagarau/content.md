# Actions, Consequences, Agency, and Consent Checklist

Use this checklist before drafting, during deterministic evaluation, and after rendering. A checked box means evidence was inspected—not that a governed consent decision is legally valid.

## 1. Authority and Evidence

- [ ] The governing product state and action behavior have named sources.
- [ ] Policy, consent, rights, and specialist owners are identified where applicable.
- [ ] Unknown facts and assumptions are recorded separately.
- [ ] The available-option inventory has provenance.
- [ ] The interaction's locale, accessibility, risk, and affected populations are known.
- [ ] No book-derived heuristic is being treated as law, policy, or runtime fact.

## 2. Actors and Scope

- [ ] Speaker/operator is explicit.
- [ ] Acting person, account owner, subject, payer, recipient, and affected parties are distinguished where needed.
- [ ] Delegate or representative authority is evidenced.
- [ ] Pronouns have one resolvable referent.
- [ ] The action object and scope are specific.

## 3. Action Contract

- [ ] The committing control names its actual action.
- [ ] The action maps to one evidenced state transition.
- [ ] Immediate, pending, partial, scheduled, and complete states are not conflated.
- [ ] Timing, duration, recurrence, and expiry are included when material.
- [ ] The interface does not claim success before authoritative confirmation.
- [ ] Unknown transaction outcomes do not offer unsafe retry.

## 4. Consequences

- [ ] Charges, losses, disclosures, commitments, delays, and eligibility effects are inventoried.
- [ ] Effects on other people or accounts are included.
- [ ] Material consequences appear before commitment.
- [ ] Irreversible boundaries and recovery windows are accurate.
- [ ] Consequence language is concrete rather than euphemistic.
- [ ] Rendered prominence will be reviewed separately from source-text presence.

## 5. Choice and Consent

- [ ] Each option produces a distinct, understood result.
- [ ] Independently governed purposes are not silently bundled.
- [ ] Acceptance and refusal are both explicit when consent applies.
- [ ] Default and preselection behavior is evidenced and disclosed as required.
- [ ] Refusal does not trigger an undisclosed penalty or partial acceptance.
- [ ] Closing, backing out, or dismissing does not accidentally commit.
- [ ] Optional uses are not presented as required for core service unless evidence says so.
- [ ] Specialist review is scheduled for validity and required disclosures.

## 6. Ongoing Agency

- [ ] A person can inspect the current choice or status.
- [ ] Edit, correct, reset, cancel, undo, revoke, appeal, or escalate exists as applicable.
- [ ] Each control's effect and limits are stated.
- [ ] A successful exit route exists.
- [ ] Human support or accountable remedy is present for consequential disputes.
- [ ] The interface explains what remains irreversible after revocation or cancellation.

## 7. Pressure and Presentation

- [ ] Labels describe outcomes rather than judging the person.
- [ ] No unsupported urgency, scarcity, guilt, shame, or peer pressure appears.
- [ ] Refusal is not made materially harder without an evidenced reason.
- [ ] Benefits and costs are presented at comparable specificity.
- [ ] Repeated prompting after refusal has a documented, relevant trigger.
- [ ] Tone and brevity have not removed material information.

## 8. Evaluation Disposition

- [ ] Every applicable deterministic rule has pass, fail, unknown, or not-applicable evidence.
- [ ] Material unknowns produce an evidence request and abstention.
- [ ] Hard failures block voice/tone ranking.
- [ ] Contextual findings are labeled advisory.
- [ ] Specialist findings identify the required discipline and question.
- [ ] Repair briefs preserve semantic invariants and prohibit invented facts.
- [ ] Repaired candidates are re-evaluated against the same rule-pack version.

## 9. Adverse-Context Tests

- [ ] Shared account or household device.
- [ ] Delegate, guardian, employer, or representative action.
- [ ] Interrupted or repeated decision.
- [ ] Pending, partial, duplicate, or unknown transaction outcome.
- [ ] Refusal, cancellation, revocation, and appeal.
- [ ] Screen reader, keyboard, zoom/reflow, and speech interaction as applicable.
- [ ] Locale expansion, RTL, morphology, and in-market interpretation as applicable.
- [ ] High stress, low frequency, and high consequence.
